What makes something an agent?

Explore selected works Sponsorship

Understanding humans through the agents we create, imagine, and live with.

Agenthropology is a proposed research concept: studying people by studying the relationships they build with artificial and imagined agents.

  1. Agents Systems, characters, and beings we design or imagine as acting on their own.
  3. Anthropology The study of people, culture, meaning, and everyday practice.
  5. Agency How capacity to act, choose, and decide is shared between humans and agents.

Agents times Anthropology leads to Agency.

  • Attribution

    How minds, intentions, and capabilities are perceived in agents.

  • Attachment

    How relationships, trust, and care may form between people and agents.

  • Authority

    How decisions and control are delegated, shared, or taken back.

  • Adaptation

    How people and systems change together over time.

The Agenthropology Initiative

AGENTHRO is the public name of The Agenthropology Initiative. We study agents (artificial, fictional, and imagined) as a way to understand the people who make and live with them.

Vision
Understanding humans through the agents we create, imagine, and live with.
Foundation
Agents × Anthropology → Agency
Research themes
Attribution · Attachment · Authority · Adaptation
Contact
agenthro@gmail.com

How we work

  • Human before machine. We focus on people, decisions, and relationships, not “AI magic.”
  • Research before decoration. Hypotheses, methods, and limitations are easy to find.
  • Designed for uncertainty. We say clearly what is a question, a hypothesis, or a finding.
