# DESIGN.md — AGENTHRO (The Agenthropology Initiative)

Direction extracted from *AGENTHRO Brand Identity & Cross-Project UI Design System v1.0*.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2

## Identity & Voice
- **Name:** AGENTHRO / The Agenthropology Initiative
- **Tagline:** We invent others to understand ourselves.
- **Foundation:** Agents × Anthropology → Agency
- **Tone:** Scientific clarity, human curiosity, gentle speculative imagination. Direct, precise, honest about what is a question, hypothesis, or finding. Never oversells.
- **Languages:** English (technical canon) and Thai (never forced uppercase or wide letter-spacing on Thai text).

## Palette (R-29)
- **Core brand:** Sky Blue `#3182F6`, Cloud White `#F7F9FF`, Ink Black `#101319`
- **Botanical accents (restrained):** Signal Coral `#FF684A`, Botanical Mint `#92C9A7`, Soft Peach `#FFCCAE`, Light Sky Blue `#A8C5FF`
- **Accessible UI semantics (light):** Background `#F7F9FF`, Surface `#FFFFFF`, Muted Surface `#E7F0FF`, Primary Text `#101319`, Muted Text `#475569`, Action `#195EC8` (white text), Error `#AD2F1B`, Success `#176344`, Warning `#80510C`.
- **Dark mode (supported via toggle):** Background `#101319`, Surface `#171E29`, Elevated `#202B39`, Primary Text `#F7F9FF`, Muted Text `#C8D3E4`, Action `#3182F6` (`#101319` text), Link/Focus `#A8C5FF`.

## Typography (R-06)
- **Display / UI:** Inter, Noto Sans Thai, sans-serif (Neue Haas Grotesk omitted until licensed). Chosen for neutral scientific legibility and bilingual Thai/Latin pairing.
- **Metadata / captions:** Roboto Mono, ui-monospace, monospace. Used sparingly for figure numbers, section indices, and status metadata.

## Rhythm & Composition (R-05, R-14)
- **RHYTHM 3 (varied compositions across sections):**
  - **Hero Cover:** Full-bleed Vivid Sky Blue (`#3182F6`) editorial cover with Ink Black (`#101319`) typography, orbital registration marks, and botanical horizon.
  - **01 Research:** Asymmetric 2-column editorial spread (sticky aside + typographic 3A equation specimen + 2×2 ruled 4A matrix).
  - **02 Projects:** Open editorial catalog table/ledger with tabular numerals (`01`–`03`) and crisp `1px` rules (no rounded card wrappers).
  - **03 People:** Asymmetric spread with concise editorial notice until credits are confirmed.
  - **04 Join:** Sticky left-hand guidance paired with an Ink-topped editorial application sheet.
  - **05 About:** Asymmetric 2-column reading layout (max 800 px measure).
  - **Footer:** Compact 3-part band on Ink Black.

## Motion (R-19)
- **MOTION 2:** Purposeful hover/focus transitions (160–220 ms ease-out), progressive form field reveal, and subtle one-shot scroll reveal. No endless loops or decorative pulsing. Honors `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
